This repository contains various free-to-use files relating to the text and interpretation of Scripture, and to Christian living.

Click on a file name, and you can either read it online, or go on to click the download button roughly in the upper right to save the file.

Special note with regard to the two Kergyma files: These two files are drafts for a (not currently existing) denomination. Churches should feel free to adopt or adapt them if they see fit. If the latter, the name should be changed in order to avoid confusion; if the former, such like-minded Christian should, it seems to me, seek each other out for fellowship and partnership, if possible.

Special note with regard to the Research Notes and Textual Commentary: These two files together form a companion to the readings spreadsheet for the Solid Rock Hebrew Bible (see https://github.com/jjmccollum/solid-rock-hb). I beg readers' indulgence, as these files were not originally intended for publication, and consequently are very inconsistent in whether they address a matter, in how fully they reference relevant literature, how clearly my train of thought (such as it is) is expressed, and how egregious the typos are. Still, I have been encouraged to make this material available, and I do hope that it might in some measure be helpful for those seeking to research the text of the Hebrew Bible.
